About

Hi, I'm Ana.

I grew up in Porto and now live in Sofia. The line at the top of this page is the real terrain between the two, and it's a reminder that data can show us where we come from and where we're going, and reveal what we'd never see with our eyes alone.

I've drawn cartographic and cadastral maps, assessed environmental impacts, and spent the last several years building crop analytics, learning how crops behave across every continent and season. Today I help organisations turn satellite data into evidence they can act on.
