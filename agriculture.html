Complete · Dobrudja, Bulgaria

Dobrudja: how long does the soil lie bare?

About 70,000 field-years in Bulgaria's grain belt, every clear Sentinel-2 image from 2019 to 2025, each day classed as living plants, stubble or bare soil. Fewer fields now sit bare all summer, but the drop came before the 2023 soil-cover rule. Winter is still the gap.

Every result was checked on the images: random fields judged by eye, and the method changed where it didn't hold up.

Complete · Maradi and Zinder, Niger

Southern Niger: are farmers' fields getting woodier?

Forty years of Landsat in the dry season, when only trees and shrubs stay green. 97% of the farmland is woodier than in the late 1980s, mostly low shrubs and young trees, strongest between Aguié, Tessaoua and Matamèye. Rain helped, but it doesn't explain where.

Checked on bare rock, against Sentinel-2 at 10 m and a 1 m tree map, and by eye in three places.
